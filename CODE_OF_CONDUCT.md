# Code of conduct

token-bar is a small project, and we want it to be easy and pleasant to take part in, whether you're filing your first issue or sending your tenth pull request.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different experience. Assume good intentions, and explain things instead of just correcting them.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for improving token-bar.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal information. When you paste output into an issue, remove anything private first.

## What happens when it isn't followed

Comments, issues, or pull requests that break these rules can be edited, hidden, or removed, and the people behind them can be blocked from the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior is a problem, report it by using GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or by opening a private report from the repository's Security tab. Reports are handled in confidence.
